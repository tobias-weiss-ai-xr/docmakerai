# openEduSuite

openEduSuite is an open-source digital workplace for higher education: a
federated suite of established open-source applications behind one login,
brought together for teaching, collaboration and administration.

## One Login, Many Apps

The suite is connected through a single sign-on: you sign in once with your
university account, and every app recognizes you. Changing your password
happens in one central place.

## The Apps

The apps below are the ones the reference portal at
[home.openedusuite.org](https://home.openedusuite.org) shows — see
[The Portal](/suite/portal) for the full tile list.

| Area | App | What it's for |
|------|-----|---------------|
| Email & groupware | **SOGo** | Mail, calendar, contacts and tasks |
| Files | **OpenCloud**, **Nextcloud** | Personal and shared files, share links |
| Wiki & knowledge | **XWiki** | Wikis and documentation |
| Projects | **OpenProject** | Project planning and collaboration |
| Chat & forum | **Element**, **Discourse** | Messaging and discussions |
| Diagrams | **draw.io**, **Excalidraw** | Diagrams and whiteboards |
| Learning management | **ILIAS** | Courses and e-learning |
| Video conferencing | **BigBlueButton** | Online lectures and meetings |
| Scientific writing | **Typst**, **Overleaf** | Papers in the browser, live preview and LaTeX |
| Shared notes | **HedgeDoc** | Collaborative Markdown notes |
| Events | **Indico** | Event management and calendars |
| Forms | **Grist** | Forms and table databases |
| Bookings | **calrs** | Public slot booking, no account needed |
| Digitization | **Kitodo** | Digitization workflows for collections |
| Helpdesk | **Request Tracker (RT)** | Support tickets |
| AI services | **Open WebUI**, **Dify**, **F13**, **OpenScience** | Language models, assistants, text and research workbenches |
| Automation | **n8n**, **Operaton**, **EWOKS** | Workflow automation, processes, scientific workflows |

Beyond this instance, further openEduSuite components are staged in the
project, among them BookStack, Planka, Etherpad, Moodle, JupyterHub,
RStudio, LimeSurvey, Paperless-ngx and Mayan EDMS. Not every institution
runs every app — your university's portal shows the apps available to you.

## Where to Start

1. Open your university's openEduSuite portal
2. Sign in once with your university account
3. Launch apps from the portal
4. For mail, calendar and tasks, read the [SOGo guides](/sogo6/)

## Accessibility

- The app table is a standard table: `Tab` walks cell by cell, the arrow
  keys move inside a row, `Enter` follows a link. Screen readers announce
  app name and purpose cell by cell.
- The links below are plain lists — `Tab` steps through them in reading
  order; each link name tells you where it leads. If you lose your
  orientation, `Ctrl+Home` (German keyboards: `Strg+Pos1`) returns to
  the top of the page.
