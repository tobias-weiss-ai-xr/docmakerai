Files with OpenCloud

OpenCloud is your file storage in the openEduSuite: keep documents, images and folders in your personal space, share them with others, and reach them from anywhere. Open it from the portal tile OpenCloud or go there directly — signing in works with your openEduSuite account.

Your Personal Space​

After signing in you land in your personal space: All files shows your files and folders.

The navigation offers:

  • Personal — your own files, with All files, Favorites, and Deleted files
  • Shares — files shared with you and by you
  • Spaces — shared areas for teams and projects

Adding Files​

Choose New to create or upload:

  • Folder — a new folder: the dialog asks for the Folder name, and with Create the folder lands in All files
  • Files Upload — upload one or more files from your computer
  • Folder Upload — upload an entire folder
  • Note, Markdown file, Shortcut — create these directly in the cloud

Files Upload opens your computer's file picker: choose one or more files, and they land in All files.

Working With Files​

Select a file or folder — right-click it, or open the ⋯ menu at the end of its row — and the actions appear above the table: Download, Delete, Copy to, Move to, Add to favorites. The row menu itself offers Open, Share, Copy permanent link, and more.

Sharing​

Choose Share and the share panel opens next to the file:

  • Share with people — invite people directly; with Can view they can look, and richer roles can be picked as your university enables them
  • Permanent link — copy a stable link to this exact file
  • Public links — add a link anyone with it can use (Add link); without one, the panel says so plainly ("This folder has no public link.")

Shares​

Under Shares you find everything that is shared:

  • Shared with me — files and folders colleagues shared with you
  • Shared with others — what you have shared
  • Shared via link — your public share links

Deleted files first go to Deleted files — from there you can restore them or remove them permanently.
