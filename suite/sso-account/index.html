Your Account

The account console manages everything about your openEduSuite login: your profile, your password, two-factor authentication, and the devices you are signed in on.

Open it from any signed-in app, or go to your account page directly and sign in when asked.

Personal Info​

Shows your account details:

  • Username and Email
  • First name and Last name

Your username and name are managed by your university; contact your helpdesk if something is wrong there.

Signing In​

Two sections control how you sign in:

  • Basic authentication: choose Update to change your password
  • Two-factor authentication: choose Set up Authenticator application to add a second factor

Two-factor authentication pairs your login with an authenticator app on your phone: after entering your password, you also type a code from the app. We recommend setting it up — it protects your account even if someone learns your password.

Device Activity​

Lists the devices and browsers where you are signed in. For each entry you can choose Sign out to end that single session, or Sign out all devices to end every session at once.

Use it when you finish working on a shared computer, or when you want to be safe after using a device you do not control.

Accessibility​

ActionKey
Move through form fields and buttonsTab
Activate a button or save a changeEnter
  • The account pages are ordinary forms and lists: Tab moves through them in order, Enter activates the focused button. Screen readers announce the field labels and headings ("Personal info", "Signing in") as you move.
  • If you lose your place, press Ctrl+Home (German keyboards: Strg+Pos1) to return to the top of the page, then use Tab to proceed from there.
