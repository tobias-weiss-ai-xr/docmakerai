openEduSuite

openEduSuite is an open-source digital workplace for higher education: a federated suite of established open-source applications behind one login, brought together for teaching, collaboration and administration.

One Login, Many Apps​

The suite is connected through a single sign-on: you sign in once with your university account, and every app recognizes you. Changing your password happens in one central place.

Documentation Status​

  • Helpdesk: Create a Ticket — the support ticket system (Request Tracker)
  • Signing in and your account: Signing In and Your Account — one login for all apps, password changes, two-factor authentication
  • SOGo (mail, calendar, contacts, tasks) is fully documented here: start with SOGo 6 or SOGo 5
  • Guides for the other apps are being added step by step as they go live

Where to Start​

  1. Open your university's openEduSuite portal
  2. Sign in once with your university account
  3. Launch apps from the portal
  4. For mail, calendar and tasks, read the SOGo guides
