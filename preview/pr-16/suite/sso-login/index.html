Signing In: One Login for All Apps

The openEduSuite apps — mail, files, wiki, helpdesk and more — share one login. You sign in once with your university account, and every app recognizes you afterwards.

How Signing In Works​

  1. Open an openEduSuite app, or open your university's portal
  2. You are forwarded to the openEduSuite login screen
  3. Enter your Username or email and your Password
  4. Choose Sign In
  5. You are returned to the app — signed in

The same login screen appears for every app. Signing in to a second app usually happens automatically, without asking again.

Managing Your Account​

After signing in, you can review and change your account details — name, password, two-factor authentication, and where you are signed in — in the account console. See Your Account.

Signing Out​

  • In most apps, sign out from the app's user menu
  • To end your session everywhere, use Sign out all devices in the account console

Accessibility​

Sign inEnter

  • The login form is a standard form: Tab moves from field to field, and Enter submits it. Screen readers announce the field labels ("Username or email", "Password") when you move into them.

  • If you are unsure where you are on the page, press Ctrl+Home (German keyboards: Strg+Pos1) to jump to the top, then use Tab to reach the form.

  • High contrast and dark mode: contrast and dark themes come from the operating system or browser — Windows Win+Ctrl+C, macOS Increase contrast, or a browser extension such as Dark Reader. If the app offers its own dark theme, switch it in its settings; the navigation described above still applies.
