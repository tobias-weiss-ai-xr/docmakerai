The Portal

The portal is the entry point of the openEduSuite: your digital workplace. You can read it without signing in — it then shows the weather for Marburg, the university news, and the events calendar. The language selector in the top bar switches the portal between Deutsch, English, Français and 中文.

Signing In​

Choose Anmelden in the top bar (Login when the portal runs in English) and sign in with your openEduSuite account — the same login that works everywhere in the suite. See Signing In: One Login for All Apps for details.

After signing in, the portal shows your personal digital workplace: the app tiles plus weather, news and events. The top bar now holds Konto (Account in English), which opens the account console in a new tab, and Abmelden (Logout), which signs you out.

The App Tiles​

Signed in, the portal sorts its tiles under two headings:

  • Dienste (Services) — the apps of the suite itself
  • Universität (University) — tiles that lead out of the suite, to services of the university

Two buttons above the grid switch the view: Kacheln (Tiles) is the grid you see first, 3D-Ansicht (3D view) lays the tiles out as a scene you can turn and zoom — the portal says "Drag to rotate · Scroll to zoom · Click to open a service". The search field Dienste filtern … (Filter services…) hides every tile that does not match your text while you type.

Tile names follow the interface language, so the tables below give both spellings. Where we have a guide, it is linked.

Universität (University)​

These tiles leave the suite for services of the university, so they have no guide here: MARVIN, the campus management system; ILIAS, the university-wide learning platform (the suite's own ILIAS is the Lernplattform (ILIAS) tile above, described in Learning platform (ILIAS)); next.hessenbox for cloud sync and share; the Personenverzeichnis staff directory; and BigBlueButton (Uni), the university's own video conference instance.

Not every tile is for everyone: what you see depends on your role and on which services your institution runs. If a service you need is missing or stays dark, ask at the helpdesk.

Accessibility​

ActionKey
Move through links and tilesTab
Open the focused link or appEnter

  • The portal is built from ordinary links and tiles: Tab moves you through them in reading order, Enter opens the focused element. The filter field Dienste filtern … takes plain text — type a service name and the grid narrows to the matching tiles.

  • Screenreader users reach the app list under the headings Dienste and Universität; every tile name is a heading of its own, sitting inside the link that opens the service.

  • If you lose your orientation, press Ctrl+Home (German keyboards: Strg+Pos1) to return to the top of the page.

  • High contrast and dark mode: contrast and dark themes come from the operating system or browser — Windows Win+Ctrl+C, macOS Increase contrast, or a browser extension such as Dark Reader. If the app offers its own dark theme, switch it in its settings; the navigation described above still applies.
