Video Conferences (BigBlueButton)

The openEduSuite's conferencing runs on BigBlueButton with Greenlight as its front door: your personal conference rooms, ready to start, for lectures, seminars, and working meetings — in the browser, no installation.

Signing In​

BigBlueButton greets you in German ("Willkommen in BigBlueButton"). Open the BigBlueButton tile in the portal, choose Anmelden (sign in) in the top right — the openEduSuite sign-in follows, and you land in your rooms.

Your Rooms​

The Rooms page is your home base: each room is a standing conference space with its own link. + New Room adds another — one for the lecture, one for office hours, one for the project.

  • Start opens the live session: microphone and camera are asked for when you join, and the conference window offers screen sharing, chat, and a collaborative whiteboard
  • Recordings keeps the sessions you recorded, ready to share with those who missed them

Share a room's link and people can join without an account — handy for guests outside the university.

If the service stays closed to you, the helpdesk reaches its operators.

Accessibility​

ActionKey
Move through the room list and buttonsTab
Open the focused actionEnter

  • Greenlight's pages are ordinary web elements: Tab moves you through rooms and actions, Enter opens the focused one.

  • Inside a running conference, keyboard reachability is limited; if you lose your orientation there, Escape leaves full-screen views, and the browser's Ctrl+Home (German keyboards: Strg+Pos1) returns you to the top of the page outside it.

  • High contrast and dark mode: contrast and dark themes come from the operating system or browser — Windows Win+Ctrl+C, macOS Increase contrast, or a browser extension such as Dark Reader. If the app offers its own dark theme, switch it in its settings; the navigation described above still applies.
