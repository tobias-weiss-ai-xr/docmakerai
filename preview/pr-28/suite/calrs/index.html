Bookings (calrs)

calrs is the openEduSuite's booking service — "Powered by calrs", says the footer of every page — from cal.rs. Reach it through the portal tile Bookings (calrs). Every member has a public booking page: colleagues and guests pick a free slot there, no account required.

Booking an Appointment​

You do not sign in to book. Open the host's booking page — its address follows the pattern

https://calendar.openedusuite.org/u/<name>/vc

where <name> is the host's account name (dots become dashes: andreas.simon@… → /u/andreas-simon/). Two event types are ready for every member:

  • Video Consultation (vc) — 30 minutes
  • Test-Termin (test) — 15 minutes, for trying it out

The page shows the host's name, the event type with its length, a timezone picker, and a month calendar: pick a day, pick a free slot, enter name and email, and confirm. Free slots appear only once the host has connected a calendar (see Sources below).

Your Own Booking Page​

On the login page choose Sign in with SSO and authenticate with your openEduSuite account. The dashboard opens: the sidebar groups Overview, Event Types, Bookings, and Teams under Scheduling, followed by Invite Links, Sources, Profile & Settings, and Troubleshoot.

  • Overview — your public page address, a getting-started checklist (connect a calendar, create your first event type, share your booking link), and counters for event types, upcoming bookings, pending approvals, and calendar sources.
  • Event Types — your bookable meeting types, each listed with its length and its public link, plus + New for another one. The edit form covers title, slug, description, location, duration, slot interval, and per-day availability hours.
  • Bookings — what others booked with you, upcoming first.
  • Teams — booking types shared across several people; teams are created by administrators, who add you as a member.
  • Invite Links — single-use booking links for internal event types, for specific contacts — no one else can book.
  • Sources — the CalDAV connection: pick a provider (SOGo, Nextcloud, Zimbra, and more) and calrs checks that calendar before a slot is offered.
  • Troubleshoot — a day view that shows, hour by hour, why a slot is available or blocked: calendar event, booking, outside hours, buffer or minimum notice.

Under Profile & Settings you set your display name, username (the part after /u/ in your booking URL), title and bio for the public page, booking email, timezone, and your default working hours. Your own page is at /u/<your name>/vc — share the link in your email signature or the wiki, and people book you without a single email thread.

Bookings and SOGo​

calrs manages the booking flow; the calendar underneath speaks CalDAV. The Sources provider list includes SOGo itself: connect your SOGo calendar and calrs checks it before offering a slot — a booked slot blocks the time in SOGo's calendar view too, and free/busy stays honest in both directions.

If the service stays closed to you, the helpdesk reaches its operators.

Accessibility​

ActionKey
Move through days, slots, and the booking formTab
Open or pick the focused elementEnter

  • The booking page is a plain page in reading order: month calendar, slot list, then the form for name and email — Tab walks you through it, Enter picks.

  • The dashboard uses ordinary links and tables; Escape closes dialogs. Ctrl+Home (German keyboards: Strg+Pos1) returns you to the top of the page.

  • High contrast and dark mode: contrast and dark themes come from the operating system or browser — Windows Win+Ctrl+C, macOS Increase contrast, or a browser extension such as Dark Reader. If the app offers its own dark theme, switch it in its settings; the navigation described above still applies.
